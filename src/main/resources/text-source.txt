小女孩每晚都对着窗外最亮的星星许愿。多年后，她成了一名宇航员。当她终于飞向太空时，发现那颗星星其实是一颗即将熄灭的恒星。她微笑着说：“谢谢你陪我长大。”

A little girl made wishes every night to the brightest star outside her window. Years later, she became an astronaut. When she finally flew into space, she discovered that star was actually a dying sun. She smiled and said, "Thank you for growing up with me."

老钟表匠修了一辈子钟表，却从不让店里的那座老钟走快一秒。有人问他为什么，他说：“我在等一个人，她说会回来取这只表。我怕钟走快了，她会觉得来晚了。”

An old watchmaker repaired clocks his whole life, but never let the old clock in his shop run a second fast. When asked why, he said, "I'm waiting for someone. She said she'd come back for this watch. I'm afraid if the clock runs fast, she'll think she's late."

邮差每天送信时，都会在路边撒下花种。十年后，他退休那天，整条路开满了鲜花。村民们说：“我们从未收到过花，却收到了最美的礼物。”

A postman scattered flower seeds along the road every day while delivering mail. Ten years later, on his retirement day, the whole road was in full bloom. The villagers said, "We never received flowers, but we received the most beautiful gift."

老教授退休前的最后一堂课，教室里坐满了学生。他写下：“知识会过时，但好奇心不会。”然后他放下粉笔，深深鞠了一躬，说：“现在，换你们去提问了。”

On the last class before retirement, the old professor's classroom was packed. He wrote: "Knowledge fades, but curiosity never does." Then he put down the chalk, bowed deeply, and said, "Now it's your turn to ask questions."

下雨天，老人修伞从不收钱，只让客人答应：下次帮别人一次。多年后，全城下雨时，总有人递伞给陌生人。老人早已去世，可雨里全是他的影子。

On rainy days, the old umbrella repairman never took money. He only asked customers to promise to help someone else once. Years later, when it rained across the city, someone was always offering a stranger an umbrella. The old man was long gone, yet his shadow was in every drop.

面包师每天多烤一个面包，放在窗台。一只流浪猫来吃。后来，猫叼来一只受伤的小鸟。面包师笑了：“原来善意也会带朋友来。”

The baker baked one extra loaf every day and left it on the windowsill. A stray cat came to eat. Later, the cat brought an injured bird. The baker smiled: "So kindness brings friends too."

灯塔看守人几乎失明，每晚仍爬上塔顶。有人问为什么。他说：“我看不见光，但船需要看见我。”

The lighthouse keeper was nearly blind, yet every night he still climbed to the top. Someone asked why. He said, "I can't see the light, but the ships need to see me."

画家画了一扇门，却从不画门把手。他说：“留给你自己推开。”他去世后，人们在他画布后发现一扇真门。

A painter painted a door but never a handle. He said, "I'll leave it for you to push open." After he died, people found a real door behind his canvas.

书店有只猫，总睡在最旧的书上。顾客问老板为什么。老板说：“它在替那些没人读的故事，暖一暖。”

A bookshop cat always slept on the oldest books. A customer asked the owner why. The owner said, "It's warming up the stories no one reads anymore."

旅人快渴死时发现一口井，旁边写着：“先倒一杯水进去，再打水。”他犹豫后照做，清泉涌出。井边还有一行小字：“信任也是水。”

A dying traveler found a well. A sign read, "Pour one cup in first, then draw water." He hesitated, obeyed, and fresh water rose. Small words beside it said, "Trust is water too."
